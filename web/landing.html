How it works

Three tiers. The cloud is last — and only ever sees the redacted version.

Perception and redaction live on your machine. The local server orchestrates and guards. The cloud planner gets placeholders, never your data.

TIER 1 · EXTENSION — ON YOUR DEVICE

Perceive, redact, execute.

A Chrome MV3 extension walks the DOM and accessibility tree, and runs YOLO, OCR and face detection on-device in an offscreen document. The redactor, the vault and the gate run here too. Florence-2 phrase grounding on WebGPU is implemented but not live-verified (no WebGPU in the sandbox) — it is being verified on the demo laptop, so the page does not claim it as proven.

DOM walker · YOLO · OCR · BlazeFaceFlorence-2 · lazy, WebGPU only
TIER 2 · LOCAL SERVER — LOCALHOST ONLY

Orchestrate, guard, remember.

A FastAPI server bound to 127.0.0.1:8000 with token auth runs the loop, the second PII scan, the vault and memory. One SQLite file (WAL) holds tasks and memory; the vault is a separate Fernet-encrypted file, never inside SQLite. RAG over your own notes runs here too — PII text is refused, and forget actually deletes.

Server binds to127.0.0.1 only
VaultFernet · PBKDF2 · file mode 0600
Per-task vault grant15-min expiry · card wiped on expiry
Cloud fallback from local modenever silent
TIER 3 · CLOUD PLANNER — REDACTED INPUT ONLY

Plan the next step. Nothing else.

An open-weights model plans the next action from the redacted observation only — Gemma, Nemotron and Mistral through our own gateway, then OrcaRouter free, then Groq, then a local Ollama model. Proprietary models are never the runtime planner. Every plan is validated before anything executes, and the executor verifies by re-observing the page.

Redacted observation→Open-weights planner→Validate→Guarded execute→Verify
What it does

Five flows, one privacy pipeline.

Every flow runs the same six steps. The ones that send, pay or submit stop at the gate and wait for you.

Job hunt

Share a resume once. Eclipse searches Internshala, LinkedIn and Unstop, shortlists with match reasons, applies only after your approval, and records a receipt per role.

Approval before every submitReceipt per role

Cold mail

Drafts one short personal mail per recruiter from the real shortlist. One approval sends one draft — nothing goes out in bulk, and a bounced address is blocked.

One approval · one sendResume attached, opt-in

Secure checkout & login

Card and login are filled locally from the encrypted vault, after your approval. The full card, CVV and OTP never leave the device, never reach the planner, never enter logs.

Card · CVV · OTP stay localVault grant · 15 min

Scanned documents

Reads PNG scans with on-device vision only. There is no DOM to walk on a scan, so the DOM score is zero by design — the on-device models do the reading.

OCR on-deviceNo DOM · by design

Live monitoring

Watches result portals and the inbox, alerts on change, and builds a morning briefing at 08:00. A watch that misses three times is marked broken and alerts once — it never silently stops.

Checks every 15+ minBriefing · 08:00

Human-only, always

CAPTCHA, 3-D Secure, passkeys and second-device OTPs are human-only. Eclipse pauses, saves a checkpoint and asks you. No solver is built, and none will be.

Pauses · checkpoints · asks you

Card numbers, CVV, passwords and OTPs never reach the planner, the logs or the cloud. They are filled locally from an encrypted vault, after your approval. FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Does Eclipse send my data to the cloud?

Only the redacted observation. Raw personal data is redacted on-device before any cloud call, and a second scan runs before the planner sees anything. The planner gets placeholders and page structure — never your values.

Which models plan the actions?

Open-weights models only — Gemma, Nemotron and Mistral through our own gateway, then OrcaRouter free, then Groq, then a local Ollama model. Proprietary models are never the runtime planner.

What happens when it hits a CAPTCHA?

It pauses, saves a checkpoint, and asks you. CAPTCHA, 3-D Secure, passkeys and OTPs are human-only — no solver is built.

Can I check what it did?

Yes: what-was-sent, egress audit, redaction proof, and a hash-chained audit log you can verify. The hash chain proves tampering after the fact; it does not prevent it.

Does it work without the cloud at all?

Yes — local mode plans with a local Ollama model (qwen2.5:7b) and never silently falls back to the cloud. If you are in local mode, you stay in local mode.

Where do I run it?

On your own machine: clone the repo, start the local server, load the extension unpacked in Chrome, and open the workspace at localhost:8000. Setup is ten steps in the README — no account, no hosted service.
